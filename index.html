Abstract

User Generated Content is all around us in the modern ages of the interconnected “web”. While the term may bring upon immediate thoughts of entertainment purposes, the actual concept of User Generated Content plays a important role in a lot of the aspects of the internet we know and use today. In this presentation, we define what User Generated Content is on a broad scale, and its role in the current web and computational landscape. We talk about potential misuses of the concept, as well as the concept being used as a everyday tool in various specific roles in our internet-oriented lives. Finally, we discuss the future of User Generated Content, and how it may be altered by growing technological changes. The information here is by no means an entire representation of the concept. User Generated Content is a far spanning tool, with multiple niche working elements that would take ages to discuss. However, this knowledge and research is compiled with the best general take on UGC and the most important bits and pieces for anyone to know.
